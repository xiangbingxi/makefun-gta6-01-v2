SOLMAR BAY

An island city that never sleeps.

You are Rio, a wheelman with nothing but a fast pair of hands and a faster car. Luna Reyes runs La Luna Bar and the jobs that come with it. Eight jobs stand between you and the Torre bank — and the Bay Police Department stands between you and all of it.

WASD move / drive  ·  mouse aim  ·  click shoot  ·  E enter car  ·  Space handbrake  ·  R radio

On foot: WASD to walk, Shift to sprint, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to steal the nearest car, 1–4 to switch weapons.

Driving: W accelerate, S brake / reverse, A/D steer, Space handbrake, H horn, E to get out.

Heat: stealing cars, shooting and speeding near cops raises your wanted level. Hide to cool off, or pay for a paint job at Spray & Shine to lose the cops.

Missions: follow the yellow marker. Talk to Luna at La Luna Bar to start the next job. The game autosaves after every job.
